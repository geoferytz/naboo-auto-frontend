NABOO AUTOMOTIVE
COMPLETE WEBSITE BUILDING ROADMAP
=================================

Project: Naboo Automotive
Target market: Tanzania
Currency: Tanzanian Shillings (TZS)
Brand style: Premium black, white, and automotive red
Frontend: Angular Standalone + TypeScript
Backend: Java + Spring Boot + GraphQL
Database: PostgreSQL
Deployment: Docker + Docker Compose + Nginx + HTTPS
Primary domain: https://naboo.co.tz
Secondary domain: https://www.naboo.co.tz

DOCUMENT PURPOSE
----------------
This roadmap defines the planned phases, deliverables, dependencies, acceptance
criteria, and production-readiness requirements for building the Naboo Automotive
website and business management platform. It is intended for a developer or
coding AI and should be used as a project execution checklist.

============================================================
1. PROJECT VISION AND OBJECTIVES
============================================================

Build a professional, mobile-first automotive platform that lets customers:
- Browse vehicles for sale and submit purchase enquiries.
- Browse rental vehicles, view rates, check availability, and make reservations.
- Book vehicle repairs and routine maintenance.
- Request vehicle painting, color changes, detailing, and restoration.
- Browse automotive accessories, view prices, and request purchases or installation.
- Request quotations, receive booking references, and track booking progress.
- Pay online through an approved payment provider or choose to pay in person.
- Use the website from mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

The business must be able to manage the full operation through a secure admin
dashboard without editing source code.

Core principles:
- Mobile-first, responsive user experience.
- Premium and consistent visual design.
- Clear pricing and booking policies.
- Secure customer information and payment processing.
- Reliable availability and inventory controls.
- Maintainable architecture and documented deployment.
- No fake availability, testimonials, reviews, or prices.
- No production features represented by hardcoded demo data.

4. FUNCTIONAL MODULES
============================================================

4.1 Vehicle sales
- Create, edit, publish, unpublish, reserve, and mark vehicles sold.
- Store make, model, variant, year, mileage, fuel type, transmission, color,
  condition, engine capacity, features, description, and optional registration data.
- Upload and order multiple images with alt text.
- Search and filter by make, model, year, price, mileage, fuel, and transmission.
- Show sale price, status, and vehicle specifications.
- Accept enquiries and viewing requests.
- Keep enquiry history and assign enquiries to sales staff.
- Ensure sold or unavailable vehicles cannot be incorrectly advertised as available.

4.2 Car rental
- Maintain rental-eligible vehicles and rental rates.
- Configure daily, weekly, and monthly rates where applicable.
- Collect pickup/return date and time and location.
- Configure extras, delivery fees, deposits, mileage policy, fuel policy,
  driver requirements, cancellation rules, and late-return rules.
- Calculate an estimated total on the backend.
- Check date-range availability before accepting a reservation.
- Prevent overlapping reservations for the same vehicle.
- Support temporary holds with configurable expiry.
- Track pending, awaiting payment, confirmed, active, returned, cancelled,
  expired, and other required states.
- Record pickup and return inspections, mileage, fuel, damage, and condition.
- Handle extensions and approved changes safely.
- Send booking references and notifications.

4.3 Repairs and maintenance
- Offer services such as general servicing, oil changes, diagnostics, brakes,
  suspension, electrical work, and other configured workshop services.
- Let customers provide vehicle details, notes, preferred date, and photos.
- Configure workshop hours, holidays, technician schedules, bays, capacity,
  and service durations.
- Show fixed prices where scope is fixed; otherwise show estimates or quote requests.
- Prevent overbooking through backend checks and database-safe concurrency controls.
- Create a workshop job card after the booking reaches the configured stage.
- Track assigned technician, labour, parts, status, and expected completion.
- Require customer approval for additional work outside the approved quotation.
- Track completed work and vehicle collection.

4.4 Painting, color change, detailing, and restoration
- Describe available services and scope.
- Let customers select an optional preferred color and upload vehicle photos.
- Request an inspection when required.
- Create itemized quotations for labour, parts, materials, and other charges.
- Record quotation validity, approval/rejection, and revisions.
- Track scheduled, in-progress, awaiting approval, completed, and collected states.
- Publish before-and-after gallery images only with appropriate authorization.

4.5 Accessories
- Manage product categories, SKU, brand, compatibility, images, selling price,
  installation price, stock quantity, and publication status.
- Provide search, filtering, and product detail pages.
- Support enquiries or a cart/order workflow.
- Check and update inventory safely.
- Track order, payment, fulfilment, cancellation, and return states as required.
- Allow installation appointments subject to workshop capacity.

4.6 Customer portal
- Show upcoming and previous bookings.
- Show rental terms and reservation details.
- Show service appointments and job status.
- Show quotations awaiting approval.
- Show invoices, payment history, and order status.
- Maintain customer profile and notification preferences.
- Support secure booking tracking and policy-based cancellation/rescheduling.

4.7 Admin dashboard
- Overview cards for sales enquiries, rental reservations, workshop jobs,
  quotations, low-stock products, payments, and outstanding invoices.
- Search, filter, sort, paginate, and export records where authorized.
- Manage catalogues, prices, users, roles, bookings, jobs, orders, and settings.
- Use clear status histories and audit records.
- Distinguish gross sales, collected payments, outstanding balances, refunds,
  and booking value according to a documented reporting policy.

9. PRICING AND MONEY RULES
============================================================

Rental formula:
Total = (Billable Days x Applicable Rate)
        + Extras
        + Fees
        - Discounts
        + Applicable Taxes, according to the configured tax policy

Business decisions required:
- How partial rental days are charged.
- Daily versus weekly/monthly rate selection.
- Deposit rules.
- Included mileage and excess mileage.
- Fuel policy.
- Delivery/collection fees.
- Late-return rules.
- Cancellation and refund policies.
- Tax display and inclusion rules.
- Service price validity and quotation expiry.

Pricing types:
- FIXED
- STARTING_FROM
- HOURLY
- INSPECTION_REQUIRED
- QUOTE_REQUIRED

Money rules:
- Never use floating-point arithmetic for financial calculations.
- Use integer minor units or PostgreSQL NUMERIC with a defined scale.
- Store currency explicitly, defaulting to TZS.
- Persist a price breakdown when an order, booking, or invoice is created.
- A catalogue price change must not silently change an existing confirmed booking.
- Restrict discounts and manual adjustments to authorized roles.
- Audit manual financial adjustments.

============================================================
11. SECURITY AND PRIVACY
============================================================

- Use secure authentication and modern password hashing.
- Apply least-privilege authorization.
- Protect privileged accounts with multi-factor authentication where practical.
- Use HTTPS and secure session/token handling.
- Validate and sanitize inputs.
- Restrict uploads by type, size, and content; use safe generated storage names.
- Keep private files private and use authenticated downloads or expiring signed URLs.
- Protect against SQL injection, XSS, CSRF where applicable, IDOR, and abuse.
- Rate-limit login, booking, and public enquiry operations.
- Keep credentials outside source control.
- Never store raw card data.
- Avoid logging tokens, passwords, or unnecessary personal information.
- Apply retention and deletion rules consistent with applicable Tanzanian requirements.
- Audit price changes, refunds, permissions, booking changes, and inventory adjustments.

============================================================
12. PAYMENTS AND NOTIFICATIONS
============================================================

Evaluate payment providers based on:
- Availability to a Tanzania-registered business.
- Mobile-money and card support.
- Settlement arrangements and fees.
- API documentation and webhook security.
- Refund and reconciliation support.
- Onboarding and production credentials.

Payment lifecycle:
- Pending
- Awaiting confirmation
- Successful
- Failed
- Expired
- Refunded
- Partially refunded, where supported

Rules:
- Never trust a browser redirect as proof of payment.
- Verify provider callbacks and transaction amounts server-side.
- Match currency, merchant account, reference, amount, and status.
- Process callbacks idempotently.
- Reconcile internal transactions against provider records.
- Support authorized in-person payment recording with audit history.
- Keep booking status separate from payment status.

Notifications:
- Email for confirmations, quotations, invoices, and important status updates.
- SMS provider adapter for critical notifications.
- Optional WhatsApp contact or approved API integration.
- Log attempts and retry transient failures.
- Notification failure must not undo a valid booking.
